The sources give different meanings for the same motif. Each meaning here comes with the source that states it, and none are merged; the <SiteLink to="sources/">sources</SiteLink> page lists them all.

## Sun, moon and stars

Across Al Jazeera, Kurdistan Chronicle and The Amargi, the sun (_roj_) is called wisdom (by Fatê Temel), protection from the evil eye (by Saliha Özşavlı), "the most important symbol for Kurdish people" (by Ali Ozmen), purity and new beginnings, and life, fertility and divine order <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023 kurdistan-chronicle-2026 amargi-2025" />.

The sun, moon, stars and peacock are said to come from Ezidi belief <Cite ids="hilton-tattooed-women dailysabah-2016" />, and the sun, moon and star are the motifs most requested today <Cite ids="sbs-2022" />. Özgür Politika says a nine-dot sun disc on a man's right temple stands for good sense, and the sun and moon together for eternal life <Cite ids="ozgur-2021" />. A moon near the eye is said to mark a conversion from Ezidism to Islam <Cite ids="natgeo-2015 mee-2022 wikipedia-deq" />.

## Protection and healing

A cross is said to ward off the evil eye or evil spirits <Cite ids="ozgur-2021 wikipedia-deq kurdistan-chronicle-2026" />, a dot between the eyes to protect against the evil eye (_nazar_) <Cite ids="wikipedia-deq" />, and an eye to turn the evil eye away <Cite ids="dailysabah-2016" />. Dots at the temples are said to relieve headaches or migraines <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023 kurdistan-chronicle-2026 natakallam-2024" />.

## Dots

Three dots stand for loyalty, according to Al Jazeera <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023" />. The Amargi is more specific: three dots on the left palm mean breaking ties, and on the right palm loyalty <Cite ids="amargi-2025" />. Özgür Politika says three dots between the thumb and index finger were given to boys to keep death away <Cite ids="ozgur-2021" />.

## Animals and plants

The gazelle (_xezal_) stands for luck <Cite ids="dailysabah-2016" />, for beauty and grace <Cite ids="wikipedia-deq" />, or for grace and feminine wisdom <Cite ids="amargi-2025" />. Vines and plant dots stand for fertility and strength, circles for fertility and lozenges for strength <Cite ids="wikipedia-deq kurdistan-chronicle-2026" />. A tree of life from the neck to the chest is tied to belief in a Mother Goddess <Cite ids="ozgur-2021" />, and a millipede stands for diligence in the house <Cite ids="dailysabah-2016" />.

## Objects

The comb stands for beauty <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023" /> or strength <Cite ids="kurdistan-chronicle-2026" />, and the five-line comb for a "second hand" <Cite ids="amargi-2025" />. A ladder on the hands stands for spiritual ascension, according to an Al Jazeera caption <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023" />. The Amargi reads the mirror as the evil eye, the rebab as peace and the crown as nobility <Cite ids="amargi-2025" />, and a crown (_taç_) is named in a song listed in the deq-ttt archive <Cite ids="deq-ttt-archive" />.

## Face

An inverted V on the chin is described as a tribal mark sized to the family group, which "lets the sweetness out" when its wearer speaks <Cite ids="natgeo-2015 mee-2022" />. Independent Türkçe quotes a speaker who did not know what the inverted fork (_ters çatal_) on the forehead means <Cite ids="indyturk-2020" />. Özgür Politika says the lip deq began as a protest at Mecca <Cite ids="ozgur-2021" />.

## Names of the motifs

NaTakallam gives Kurmanji names: _roj_ (sun), _heyv_ (moon), _stêrin_ (stars), _xezal_ (gazelle), _marin_ (snakes), _rewik_ (plants), _meqes_ or _cawbirr_ (scissors) and _bûkoke_ (dolls) <Cite ids="natakallam-2024" />. Özgür Politika lists the shapes women wear: birds, the gazelle, the dot, the triangle, the octagon, the square, a divided rectangle and dotted geometric shapes; men wear the scorpion, the snake, the pistol and the sword <Cite ids="ozgur-2021" />.
