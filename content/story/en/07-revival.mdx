## Fatê Temel

Fatê (Fatma) Temel did her first deq in 2018 in Derik, and since November 2021 she has run a studio in Sur, Diyarbakır <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023" />. Al Jazeera reported that she charged about 200 Turkish lira per deq and saw two or three customers a day <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023" />. SBS spoke with her about keeping deq alive, and its image credit spells her surname "Metel" <Cite ids="sbs-2022" />.

## Studios

Deq Art Studio works in Amed <Cite ids="instagram-deq-artstudio" />, and the artist Arjin runs the Sunya Deq studio in Istanbul <Cite ids="amargi-2025" />.

## Ahmet Yavuklu

The photographer and filmmaker Ahmet Yavuklu calls deq "a form of worship" <Cite ids="aljazeera-2023" />. His films are on his YouTube channel <Cite ids="yt-yavuklu-channel" />.
