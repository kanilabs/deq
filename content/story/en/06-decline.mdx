---
title: Decline
description: When deq stopped being made, and the reasons the sources give.
---

By the mid-1960s, about fifty years before the photo essays from Kobanî, the practice had largely stopped <Cite ids="natgeo-2015 hilton-tattooed-women" />.

The sources give several reasons. Wikipedia names religious views that deq is _haram_ and the sense that it was old-fashioned, with henna taking its place <Cite ids="wikipedia-deq" />. NaTakallam names assimilation <Cite ids="natakallam-2024" />, and Kurdistan Chronicle names stigma, migration and urbanisation <Cite ids="kurdistan-chronicle-2026" />.
