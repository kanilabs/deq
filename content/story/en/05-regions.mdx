Deq is most closely tied to Mardin, Diyarbakır (Amed) and Şanlıurfa <Cite ids="wikipedia-deq dailysabah-2016" />. Özgür Politika also names Siverek, Viranşehir, Hilvan, Derik, Mazıdağı, Kızıltepe, Ömerli and Nusaybin <Cite ids="ozgur-2021" />. From Urfa the practice reached Syria through Kobanî <Cite ids="natgeo-2015" />.

## Ezidi traditions

The sun, moon, stars and peacock in deq are said to come from Ezidi belief <Cite ids="hilton-tattooed-women dailysabah-2016" />. After E. S. Drower, Wikipedia lists these Ezidi patterns: the comb, the cross, the gazelle, _daqqayeh_, the sandgrouse foot, the moon, the doll, the spindle, _res daqq_ and _dimlich_ <Cite ids="wikipedia-deq" />.

A plate of Ezidi tattoo designs circulates online as Drower's record. It is shown here, but the book and the plate have not yet been checked <Cite ids="drower-1941" />.
