The Deq site is a progressive web app. After your first visit it works without a connection, and you can install it like an app.

## Install

- **Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers** (computer and Android): choose the install icon in the address bar, or **Install app** in the browser menu.
- **Safari on iPhone and iPad**: choose **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Safari on a Mac**: choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.

The installed app opens in its own window, without the browser's address bar.

## Offline

On the first visit the site stores its pages, styles, scripts, icons, SVG files and fonts. Then everything below works offline, in all three languages:

- the icons and icon pages,
- the guides and the story text,
- the Studio, including SVG, vector and code exports, Save, Open and My shapes,
- the Leet tool and the pattern generator.

Some things still need a connection:

- **Raster exports** (PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, ICO, favicon and PSD), because their WebAssembly encoders are loaded on demand and not stored.
- **Photos** in the story, the screenshots in these guides and the showcase previews.
- **Search** works offline only for the parts you have searched before.
- Links to other sites, such as videos and sources.

## Updates

When a new version of the site is out, a notice says "A new version of this site is available." Choose **Reload** to use it now, or **Later** to keep the current page; the new version starts the next time you open the site.

## Clearing

Your designs and My shapes are stored in the browser, not in the app. Clearing the site's data in the browser settings removes them, together with the stored pages.
