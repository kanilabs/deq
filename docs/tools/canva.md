> Based on Canva's help pages, linked below; not tested here. Corrections are welcome in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/kanilabs/deq/issues).

Canva treats an uploaded SVG as one graphic: you can resize and recolour it, but not edit its paths. Set everything else before you upload.

## Get the files

Export an SVG with the final look already set: in the [Studio](../guide/exporting.md), choose the **Stroke width**, **Colour** and **Layer colours**, then export `svg`. **Download SVG** on an icon page also works for single-colour use.

## Upload

Upload the SVG to your Canva uploads and add it to a design like any other upload.

## Recolour

Canva lets you recolour an uploaded SVG, including with your Brand Kit colours.

## Stroke and layers

Canva's help pages do not describe changing an SVG's stroke width or keeping its layers. To change the stroke, export again from the Studio with a different **Stroke width** and upload the new file. To arrange parts separately, export them as separate shapes.

## Build compositions

Build the composition in the Studio and export it as one SVG, or add several shapes at the same size and leave clear space between them. See [spacing and composition](../designer/composition.md).

## Sources
