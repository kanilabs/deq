# Deq brand

> Phase 2 filled in the flag and the logo concepts. Phase 12 added the wordmark, lockups, brand tokens, the brand guide and the kits (ADR 0023).

## Logo

- The concepts are in `assets/brand/concepts/logo-concept-*.json`, in the shape format with category `brand`, on the 24-unit grid. They are drawn mono and in flag colours in `docs/design/logo-concepts.svg` (`uv run deq-spec sheet --set concepts --force`).
- **The Deq mark is the Dot rosette** (ADR 0013, superseding concept A): `dot-rosette` with `center: "ring"` and 8 dots, inside the Kanî badge (`#F8F8F8` disc r 490, 4 px `#1E1E1E` outline on a 1024 canvas). Dots `#1D4A2E` (dark mode `#8FDBA5`), ring `#FEBD11`. Small sizes (64 px and below) use dots r 1.5 and ring 2.4 in `viewBox="3 3 18 18"`.
- Files in `assets/brand/` (`logo`, `mark`, `mark-dark`, `mark-mono`, `mark-small`, `mark-small-dark`, `mark-white`, `mark-flag`, `favicon`, banners, `social-preview`, `figma-cover`, `lockups/`, `guide/`, the animated SVGs, `brand.json`, `tokens.css`) are generated by `uv run deq-spec brand`; never edit them, and `deq-spec lint` fails when they are stale. Concepts A to D stay for reference only.

## Wordmark and lockups (ADR 0023)

- The wordmark is "Deq" in Noto Sans Bold and "دەق" in Vazirmatn Bold, as HarfBuzz outlines in `assets/brand/wordmark.json` (`uv run deq-fonts wordmark`). Never set it as live text or in another font. `just brand` runs `deq-fonts wordmark`, then `deq-spec brand` (`brand.py`, `lockups.py`).
- Proportions are fractions of the mark's diameter D: Latin cap height 0.5, Sorani height 0.75, gap 0.25, stacked gap 0.2, clear space 0.25. Minimum sizes: mark 16 px, horizontal 120 px, stacked 64 px; the small mark at 64 px and below. Values live in `brand.json`; change them in `lockups.py`, not by hand.
- Lockups: `{horizontal,stacked}[-ckb]-{color,reversed,mono,white,flag}.svg`. In Sorani lockups the mark is on the right.
- Colours: `brand.json`, `tokens.css` and `docs/design/brand-colors.md` give HEX, RGB, HSL, OKLCH, CMYK (plain conversion, no profile) and contrast on white and Night.
- The animated logo (dots clockwise from the top, then the ring, 1.7 s) must keep its `prefers-reduced-motion` still state. Videos: `just brand-video`.
- PNGs, `favicon.ico` and the brand and press kit ZIPs are built by the site (`apps/site/src/lib/brandkit.ts`); nothing is rendered with Inkscape. Store screenshots (`just store`), README clips (`just clips`) and logo videos are committed and re-recorded when the UI changes; check them by eye.
- The guide is `/brand` and `docs/design/brand.md`; keep both in step with the `brand.*` strings in `en.ts`.
- Package READMEs use the absolute banner URL `https://kanilabs.github.io/deq/brand/banner.png`.

## Flag of Kurdistan

- `spec/flag.json` is the only source for flag values, and the lint checks it against the hash-pinned Commons SVG in `spec/reference/`.
- 3:2 ratio. Equal red `#ED2024`, white `#FFFFFF` and green `#278E43` bands, with a yellow `#FEBD11` sun of 21 rays, first ray up, half the flag height, inner radius half the outer radius.
- Pantone values are `null` until verified from a primary source; never quote one.
- Use only the variants listed in `spec/flag.json` (full colour, mono black, mono white, outline, high contrast, square). Never recolour, stretch or change the ray count of the flag itself.

## Colour

- All colour pairs must meet WCAG AA where they carry text or meaning; `spec/flag.json` lists the measured contrast pairs.
- No pair of flag colours reaches 4.5:1. Red on white (4.35) and white on green (4.17) pass only for large text and graphics (3:1). Never use yellow on white (1.68) or red on green (1.04) to carry meaning; use black or white text instead.
